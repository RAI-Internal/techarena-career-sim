Rules
=====

This page covers only the rules participants use most when making decisions: how
to spend quarterly resources, how salary is paid, how performance works, and when
promotion windows arrive.

Quarterly energy actions
------------------------

Each quarter you have 3 ``Energy`` points. Unused energy does not carry over to
the next quarter, so hoarding it usually does not look prudent, it just means you
did less for no reason.

.. list-table:: Energy actions
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 12 58

   * - Action
     - Cost
     - Public effect
   * - Intensive work
     - 2
     - ``Output +``, ``Health -``, ``Dignity -``
   * - Skill study
     - 2
     - ``Skill +``, ``Health -``
   * - Network maintenance
     - 1
     - ``Network +``, ``Dignity +``
   * - Defuse past landmines
     - 2
     - None (or invisible organizational effects)
   * - Mental recovery
     - 1
     - ``Dignity +``
   * - Health recovery
     - 1
     - ``Health +``
   * - Prepare a report
     - 1
     - ``Output +``, ``Dignity +``

Quarterly main action
---------------------

Each quarter you may also pick 1 main action, or none. It is more like the
direction button for actively steering your career path that quarter.

.. list-table:: Main actions
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 32 68

   * - Action
     - Public effect
   * - Apply for a key project
     - ``Output +``, ``Skill +``, ``Network +``
   * - Report results to leadership
     - ``Output +``, ``Network +``, ``Dignity +``
   * - Push for resources
     - ``Output +``, ``Network +``
   * - Apply for a job rotation
     - ``Skill +``, ``Network +``
   * - Publicize your results
     - ``Output +``, ``Dignity +``
   * - Build a mentor relationship
     - ``Skill +``, ``Network +``
   * - Repair a key relationship
     - ``Network +``, ``Dignity +``
   * - Lie low and avoid risk
     - ``Health +``

Salary and performance
----------------------

Monthly salary is paid by level, and the higher the level, the faster it grows.

.. list-table:: Monthly base salary
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 24 30

   * - Level
     - Wealth / month
   * - L1
     - 0.3
   * - L2
     - 0.4
   * - L3
     - 0.5
   * - L4
     - 0.7
   * - L5
     - 1.0
   * - L6
     - 1.5
   * - L7
     - 2.3
   * - L8
     - 3.6
   * - L9
     - 5.7
   * - L10
     - 9.1

A formal performance review happens every 6 months. Performance is decided by
many factors, but only the grade meaning and the bonus direction are public; the
exact formula is not.

.. list-table:: Performance grades
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 12 26 18 44

   * - Grade
     - Meaning
     - Bonus change
     - How to read it
   * - S
     - Exceptional
     - ``+2``
     - The organization is saying you really delivered lately.
   * - A
     - High
     - ``+1``
     - Solid work; you were not coasting on luck this stretch.
   * - B
     - Normal
     - ``0``
     - Stable, but you have not pulled ahead yet.
   * - C
     - Low
     - ``-1``
     - Time to reflect on whether your effort went to the wrong place.
   * - D
     - Very low
     - ``-2``
     - Usually not the kind of grade a "do better next time" wipes away.

Promotion windows
-----------------

A promotion window opens every 6 months. Promotion weighs ``Skill``, ``Network``,
``Output`` and more, and the higher the level, the higher the bar.

Two points matter here and are easy to misread:

- Promotion looks at long-term results; one suddenly shiny month will not lock it
  in.
- The exact thresholds are not public, so you infer how close you are from
  performance, feedback, and long-term trends.

Put simply: the organization will not hand you a "3 points from promotion" card.
It is better at giving you a standard "keep up the good work and keep growing"
right when you thought you had it in the bag.
