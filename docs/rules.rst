Public rules
============

This page states the published rules governing salary, quarterly resources,
performance, and promotion windows, organised by cadence: monthly,
quarterly, and every six months. Exact internal formulas and thresholds are
not published.

------

higher monthly payment.

Quarterly Energy actions
------------------------

At the end of each quarter, the agent receives 3 ``Energy`` points. Energy may
be spent on the actions below until the points are exhausted or the agent ends
the phase. Unused Energy does not carry over to the next quarter.

.. list-table:: Energy actions
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 12 58

   * - Action
     - Cost
     - Public effect
   * - High-intensity work
     - 2
     - ``Output +``, ``Health -``, ``Dignity -``
   * - Skill learning
     - 2
     - ``Skill +``, ``Health -``
   * - Network maintenance
     - 1
     - ``Network +``, ``Dignity +``
   * - Defuse legacy risk
     - 2
     - No published direct statistical effect
   * - Mental recovery
     - 1
     - ``Dignity +``
   * - Health recovery
     - 1
     - ``Health +``
   * - Prepare report materials
     - 1
     - ``Output +``, ``Dignity +``

Quarterly main action
---------------------

In addition to Energy actions, the agent may select one main action per
quarter, or select no main action. The main action represents the principal
career focus for that quarter.

.. list-table:: Main actions
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 36 64

   * - Action
     - Public effect
   * - Apply for a key project
     - ``Output +``, ``Skill +``, ``Network +``
   * - Report results to your manager
     - ``Output +``, ``Network +``, ``Dignity +``
   * - Fight for resources
     - ``Output +``, ``Network +``
   * - Apply for rotation
     - ``Skill +``, ``Network +``
   * - Showcase results
     - ``Output +``, ``Dignity +``
   * - Build a mentorship
     - ``Skill +``, ``Network +``
   * - Repair a key relationship
     - ``Network +``, ``Dignity +``
   * - Lay low and reduce risk
     - ``Health +``

Performance review
------------------

A formal performance review takes place every six months. Performance is
determined by multiple factors. The grade meanings and bonus directions are
public; the exact evaluation formula is not.

.. list-table:: Performance grades
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 12 20 28 40

   * - Grade
     - Meaning
     - Half-year bonus
     - Public interpretation
   * - S
     - Exceptional
     - 3 × monthly base
     - Performance substantially above expectations.
   * - A
     - Strong
     - 2 × monthly base
     - Performance above the normal expected level.
   * - B
     - Solid
     - 1 × monthly base
     - Performance meets the normal expected level.
   * - C
     - Weak
     - 0.25 × monthly base
     - Performance below expectations; corrective action is advisable.
   * - D
     - Very weak
     - No bonus
     - Performance materially below expectations.

Promotion windows
-----------------

A promotion window opens every six months. Promotion assessment considers
factors including ``Skill``, ``Network``, and ``Output``. Requirements
increase with level, and the exact thresholds are not published.

Promotion is based on sustained results rather than a single month of unusually
strong statistics. Participants should use performance outcomes, visible
statistics, and longer-term trends returned during a run to assess progress.
No exact distance to promotion is provided.
